If you've been here before, you might notice things look a little different. Spoodly Space is now **Polly's Web** — and while the name on the door has changed, the little corner of the web you know and love hasn't.

## Why the new name?

Polly's Web is joining the Proservability family. That partnership means more resources for the things you care about: better care tools, more guides, and a longer, healthier life for the product your spoods live in. A new name marks the new chapter — a web with Polly, our resident keeper, at the center of it.

## What changes for you

Honestly? Almost nothing, and that's the point:

- Your account, spoods, care logs, photos, and stories all carry over exactly as they were.
- Your sign-in works the same as always.
- The same thoughtful care tools are right where you left them — with more on the way.

## What's coming

The family connection means we can invest in the roadmap faster: more care guides, richer dashboards, and community features that make spood keeping better together. We'll share what's landing here on the blog as it ships.

Thank you for being part of this community from the Spoodly Space days into the Polly's Web era. Happier, healthier spoods — that's the mission, whatever we're called. 🧡
