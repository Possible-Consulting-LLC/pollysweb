Our commitment to an inclusive experience.

## Our commitment

Polly's Web should work for every spood lover, regardless of ability, device, or assistive technology. We want keeping a care journal to be something anyone can do comfortably.

## What we do

- **Semantic, keyboard-friendly interfaces:** pages use real headings, links, buttons, and form labels, and are operable by keyboard.
- **Current status information:** the sign-in state of the page is communicated with roles that assistive technology recognizes.
- **Contrast and legibility:** we aim for readable text sizes and strong contrast across light and dark themes.
- **Meaningful alternatives:** images carry descriptive alternative text; icons that only decorate are hidden from assistive technology.
- **Responsive design:** the app works across phones, tablets, and desktops.

## Known limitations

We're a small team, and accessibility work is never finished. Some richer interactions are still improving, and much of the app predates this statement. When we redesign a surface, accessibility is part of the redesign.

## Feedback

If you hit a barrier anywhere on the site, please tell us at [support@pollysweb.com](mailto:support@pollysweb.com) — describe the page and what didn't work, and we will do our best to fix it quickly. Feedback directly shapes what we improve next.
