Polly's Web is powered by people who love and care for jumping spiders. These guidelines keep the community kind, helpful, and safe for everyone — keepers of every experience level, and the spoods they love.

## Our community values

### Be kind

Treat others with respect and positivity. New keepers ask beginner questions; experienced keepers share hard-won lessons. Every question deserves patience.

### Be helpful

Share knowledge, ask clarifying questions, and support each other. Care notes and shared experiences help everyone keep happier spoods.

### Be inclusive

Everyone is welcome, regardless of experience level. We learn together.

### Keep it relevant

Keep discussion focused on jumping spiders and related care topics.

## What we don't allow

- **Harm to animals:** content promoting neglect, improper care, or intentional harm to spiders or any animal.
- **Unkind conduct:** harassment, insults, shaming, or personal attacks — including toward beginners.
- **Inappropriate content:** sexual, violent, or otherwise objectionable material.
- **Misleading claims:** presenting guesses as veterinary fact, or remedies that could endanger animals.
- **Spam and selling:** unsolicited promotion, commercial advertising, or solicitation outside any features we may specifically provide for it.

## Reporting issues

If you see something that violates these guidelines, please let us know at [support@pollysweb.com](mailto:support@pollysweb.com) with details and, where possible, a link. We review reports and may remove content, warn accounts, or suspend access for violations. Serious violations of our [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use) may result in permanent suspension.

## The short version

Kind people, healthy spoods, honest information. If a post wouldn't make a new keeper feel welcome and a spood safer, it doesn't belong here.
