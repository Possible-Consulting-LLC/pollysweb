Important information and limitations about the care content on Polly's Web.

## 1. Not veterinary advice

Polly's Web provides care information, guides, reminders, and tools for jumping spider keepers. Nothing on the site is veterinary advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and nothing on the site guarantees an animal's health or outcomes. Care decisions are yours. When a spood shows signs of illness, injury, or distress beyond your experience, seek a qualified professional.

## 2. Your care, your judgment

Reminders, due-date suggestions, status labels, streaks, and badges are organizational tools. They reflect the schedules and settings you configure and general care guidance — they do not know your individual animal. Always use your own observation and judgment, and adjust care to your spood's species, age, and condition.

## 3. Accuracy and changes

We work to keep guides accurate and current, but care practices evolve and every animal is an individual. Content is provided "as is" without warranties of any kind to the extent permitted by law. See our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service) for the full limits of responsibility.

## 4. Community content

Experiences and notes shared by keepers reflect their personal views and circumstances and are not ours. Use the same judgment with community advice as with any other information.

## 5. External links

Where we link to external resources, we do so for usefulness; we don't control those sites and don't endorse their content.

## 6. Questions

If something in our care content seems wrong, tell us at [support@pollysweb.com](mailto:support@pollysweb.com) — corrections make the guides better for everyone.
